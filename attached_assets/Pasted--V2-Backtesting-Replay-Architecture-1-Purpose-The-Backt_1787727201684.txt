# V2 Backtesting & Replay Architecture

## 1. Purpose

The Backtesting & Replay Engine evaluates strategy rules on historical market data while preserving realistic chronological information flow.

The engine must simulate what would have been known at each historical timestamp.

It must never use future candles, future outcomes, later structure, later news, or historical trades created after the replay timestamp.

---

## 2. Core Principle

Historical analysis must be performed candle-by-candle.

At replay time T:

AVAILABLE:
- candles at or before T
- news known at or before T
- PDF rules already verified
- historical trades completed before T
- currency-strength data available at or before T

FORBIDDEN:
- candles after T
- final future swing highs/lows
- later MSS/BOS
- future news outcomes
- future trade results
- future historical similarity records

This is mandatory look-ahead protection.

---

## 3. Replay Modes

Support:

### BAR-BY-BAR REPLAY
Advance one completed candle at a time.

### MULTI-TIMEFRAME REPLAY
Reconstruct W, D, H4, H1, M30, M15, M5, M1 context at each timestamp.

### SETUP REPLAY
Start before a known historical setup and observe whether the system identifies it naturally.

### TRADE REPLAY
Reconstruct a stored WIN, LOSS, BREAKEVEN, or AVOIDED trade from pre-entry conditions.

### BLIND BACKTEST
Scan historical data without supplying the known future outcome.

---

## 4. Replay Inputs

Each replay request should include where applicable:

- replay_id
- instrument
- asset_class
- methodology
- date_from
- date_to
- starting timestamp
- ending timestamp
- execution timeframe
- higher timeframes
- candle count
- strategy/rule filters
- session filters
- news filter
- risk settings
- initial account balance if portfolio simulation is required

Never default to EUR_USD.

Use the exact requested supported instrument.

---

## 5. Market Data

Historical candles must come from a validated source such as OANDA or another approved feed.

Store:

- instrument
- timeframe
- timestamp
- open
- high
- low
- close
- volume where available
- candle completion state

Only completed candles should normally drive confirmed structural decisions.

---

## 6. Multi-Timeframe Synchronization

All timeframes must be synchronized to the replay timestamp.

Example:

Replay time:
10:35

M5:
10:35 candle may still be forming depending on data convention.

H1:
only the last completed H1 candle may be used for confirmed H1 structure.

D:
only the latest completed Daily candle may be treated as confirmed.

Never allow lower-timeframe replay to access a higher-timeframe candle that had not closed yet.

---

## 7. Market-State Snapshot

At every replay step generate a historical market snapshot containing:

- exact timestamp
- current price
- HTF structure
- intermediate structure
- execution structure
- liquidity state
- dealing range
- premium/discount
- POIs
- displacement
- MSS/BOS state
- relevant PDF rules
- news risk
- currency strength
- available historical similarity evidence

This snapshot represents only information known at that moment.

---

## 8. Rule Retrieval During Replay

Use the Version 2 Rule Retrieval Engine.

Retrieve only verified rules relevant to:

- methodology
- current market structure
- liquidity
- POI
- direction
- timeframe
- session

Rule retrieval itself may use the current historical market state but never future outcome information.

---

## 9. Setup Feature Extraction

At each candidate setup, generate a record conforming to:

`v2_setup_feature_schema.json`

Set:

`source_type = replay`

The record must contain only evidence available at that timestamp.

Outcome must initially remain:

`OPEN`

until future candles are replayed.

---

## 10. Candidate Setup Detection

The engine may identify a candidate when conditions such as these appear:

- relevant liquidity event
- valid POI interaction
- displacement
- meaningful MSS/BOS
- retracement
- entry trigger

Candidate detection must follow retrieved PDF methodology.

Do not scan for setups using final historical outcome.

---

## 11. Confirmation Timing

A setup becomes confirmed only when all required conditions were visible historically.

Example:

Liquidity sweep at 10:05
Displacement at 10:10
MSS confirmed at 10:15
FVG retracement at 10:25

The setup must not be marked confirmed at 10:05.

Store the actual confirmation timestamp.

---

## 12. Entry Simulation

Entry types may include:

- market after confirmation
- limit/retracement entry
- PDF-defined execution model

Record:

- signal timestamp
- entry timestamp
- entry price
- entry zone
- confirmation timeframe
- spread/slippage where modeled
- reason for entry

Do not assume perfect fills.

---

## 13. No False Precision

Historical OHLC candles do not always reveal exact intrabar sequence.

If both stop and target occur inside the same candle and lower-timeframe data is unavailable:

mark outcome as:

`AMBIGUOUS_INTRABAR`

Do not choose the favorable outcome automatically.

Use lower-timeframe data where available to resolve sequence.

---

## 14. Stop Placement

Stops must follow:

- PDF methodology
- structural invalidation
- user risk rules

Store:

- stop price
- invalidation level
- stop distance
- risk %
- stop logic

Never move a historical stop after seeing future price action unless the strategy explicitly permits management based on information available at that time.

---

## 15. Target Simulation

Track:

- TP1
- TP2
- TP3
- opposing liquidity
- HTF objective
- partial exits
- runner logic

Record whether each target was touched and at what timestamp.

Targets must be defined before their outcome is known.

---

## 16. Trade Management

Management actions may include:

- partial close
- move to breakeven
- trailing stop
- structural stop adjustment
- time-based exit
- session close exit

Every action must be triggered only by information available at that historical moment.

---

## 17. Outcome Classification

Supported outcomes:

- WIN
- LOSS
- BREAKEVEN
- AVOIDED
- OPEN
- EXPIRED
- AMBIGUOUS_INTRABAR

Also classify losses where possible:

- VALID_SETUP_NORMAL_LOSS
- INVALID_SETUP
- EARLY_ENTRY
- LATE_ENTRY
- NO_CONFIRMATION
- WEAK_MSS_BOS
- NEWS_RISK
- STOP_ERROR
- MANAGEMENT_ERROR
- EXECUTION_ERROR
- other supported classification

---

## 18. MAE and MFE

Calculate:

MAE = Maximum Adverse Excursion

MFE = Maximum Favorable Excursion

Prefer R-normalized values:

- MAE_R
- MFE_R

These help determine whether:

- stops are consistently too tight
- exits are premature
- trades frequently move close to target before reversing
- management reduces achievable R

---

## 19. R-Multiple Calculation

For each simulated trade calculate where valid:

Initial Risk = |Entry - Stop|

Realized R =
Realized Profit/Loss / Initial Risk

Track:

- planned R:R
- realized R
- maximum potential R
- MAE_R
- MFE_R

Do not fabricate R when entry/stop data is incomplete.

---

## 20. News During Replay

Historical news must be evaluated using information available at the replay timestamp.

Record:

- event
- affected currency/market
- scheduled time
- impact
- known actual/forecast only after release time
- news risk

Do not use the eventual surprise before the release occurs.

---

## 21. Currency Strength During Replay

Currency-strength calculations must use only historical candles available at that time.

Never calculate strength using future price data.

---

## 22. Historical Similarity During Replay

Similarity scoring may use only historical trades completed BEFORE the replay timestamp.

Example:

Replay date:
2025-06-01

Trade completed:
2025-07-10

Forbidden comparison.

This prevents information leakage.

---

## 23. Avoided Setup Simulation

The engine must also record setups rejected because of:

- missing confirmation
- HTF conflict
- news risk
- weak POI
- poor R:R
- late entry
- invalid structure
- excessive spread
- session restriction

Store as:

`AVOIDED`

Then replay subsequent price without altering the original decision.

---

## 24. Setup Expiry

A setup may expire if:

- POI invalidated
- structural thesis invalidated
- session ends
- entry never triggers
- relevant liquidity objective is reached first
- news conditions invalidate timing

Store the reason.

---

## 25. Backtest Modes

Support future modes:

### SINGLE SETUP
Test one PDF-defined setup.

### MULTI-SETUP
Compare multiple entry models.

### FILTER TEST
Compare setup with/without:
- news filter
- currency strength
- HTF alignment
- MSS requirement
- session filter

### PARAMETER TEST
Test permissible parameters without changing methodology.

Avoid unrestricted optimization that overfits historical data.

---

## 26. Walk-Forward Validation

Later implementation should support:

Training Period
→ Validation Period
→ Forward Test Period

Do not optimize and evaluate on the same historical sample without disclosure.

---

## 27. In-Sample vs Out-of-Sample

Clearly label:

IN_SAMPLE
OUT_OF_SAMPLE
FORWARD_REPLAY

Performance from in-sample optimization must never be presented as independent validation.

---

## 28. Costs

Where possible model:

- spread
- commission
- slippage
- financing/swap where relevant

Backtest results without costs must be labeled accordingly.

---

## 29. Portfolio Risk

Future portfolio simulation may include:

- simultaneous positions
- correlated instruments
- account drawdown
- daily loss limits
- weekly loss limits
- maximum concurrent risk

Do not assume independent trades when positions overlap.

## 34. Audit Requirements

Each replay must preserve:

- data source
- candle timestamps
- rules used
- setup features
- entry decision
- exit decision
- historical trades available at that time
- news data available at that time
- engine version

Results must be reproducible.

---

## 35. Non-Negotiable Rules

The replay engine must never:

- use future candles
- use future trade outcomes
- use future historical trades for similarity
- use unreleased news results
- retroactively improve entries
- choose favorable intrabar sequence
- move stops using hindsight
- optimize rules solely to improve past profit
- fabricate fills
- fabricate spread
- classify every historical winner as a valid strategy setup
- hide ambiguous outcomes

---

## 36. Version 2 Objective

The replay engine must eventually answer:

- Would the system have identified this setup in real time?
- At what exact point was confirmation available?
- Would the trade actually have triggered?
- What was the realistic R outcome?
- How much MAE/MFE occurred?
- Which PDF rules improved performance?
- Which conditions repeatedly preceded losses?
- Does adding news/strength/HTF filters improve historical expectancy?
- Can the results be reproduced without hindsight?
